# Phase 17 — Report, demonstration and handoff

**Status:** Not started · **Depends on:** 16 · **Owner:** Assessment/evidence lead and whole team

## Goal and entry

Deliver the supervisor's required report and demonstration using the qualified release. Assemble the evidence collected since Phase 00; do not reconstruct unrecorded measurements or claim pending hardware behavior as proven.

## Work

- [ ] Freeze the passing release/build, model/settings, firmware, dependency manifest and route. Record the actual freeze and assessment dates.
- [ ] Finish the report: problem/user context, scope, approved architecture, actual implementation, reviewed hardware/power design, UI/control flows, data/security/retention, method/results for every metric and honest limitations.
- [ ] Link requirements to measured evidence and explain failures that led to fixes. Distinguish final passes from earlier component experiments and unresolved future work.
- [ ] Prepare the supervised manual demonstration: new registration/ROI; ready state; held gesture movement/release; session Stop if ending unsuccessfully; separate PS4 DualShock 4 session; live two-room search; advisory obstacle warning; candidate pause; rejection/Resume; confirmation/Found; history after restart.
- [ ] Demonstrate gesture and PS4 DualShock 4 control and never switch mode during an active session. A Found record cannot reopen; register again for another search.
- [ ] Rehearse stop/fault recovery and the local launcher with internet disconnected, while preserving local Wi-Fi and the approved operating conditions. Use a people-clear route and available stop controls.
- [ ] Prepare fresh eligible test records for assessment; retention is not disabled to preserve old data. Keep agreed qualification recordings/report evidence separate from runtime records.
- [ ] Package setup/operation/recovery instructions, the release manifest, reviewed wiring references, source and approved evidence/report materials according to actual submission instructions.
- [ ] Perform a final cold launch and presentation rehearsal. A code/configuration change after qualification requires affected checks before it replaces the frozen release.

## Acceptance and evidence

The report/submission matches the actual passing system and requirements. The demo works from the fixed desk across connected rooms on the recorded local setup, with no hidden cloud dependency or bypassed failure gate. Submission format, deadline and recipient are taken from confirmed assessment instructions, not the reference plan.

**Save:** final report, demonstration script/recording if required, release archive/manifest, evidence index, operator instructions and confirmed submission record.

## Done

Done when the required report/demo package is delivered under the confirmed assessment process and the team can reproduce and operate the qualified release. New enhancements require a separate approved scope; they are not added as unrequested expansion phases.
